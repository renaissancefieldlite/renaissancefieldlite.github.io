Measured AI state, carried into quantum workflows.

Plain-language method

The quantum computer is the test environment. The measured relation is the object.

The workflow hides the target relation from the controller, collects only the allowed observables, attempts recovery, rejects malformed or leaking controls, and records the complete runtime and result chain. That makes the test auditable instead of decorative.

Current evidence

What the program supports today.

Problem

Simon hidden-invariant recovery

The program defines a hidden relation, exposes bounded observations, and tests whether the relation can be recovered without giving the answer to the controller.

Controls

Blind and randomized checks

Local validation uses frozen inputs, randomized larger cases, malformed controls, and leakage checks before a transport result can be accepted.

Transport

Aqora / Nexus H2-2E emulator

A frozen policy completed a bounded private blinded run through the Quantinuum H2-2E emulator route exposed through Aqora/Nexus.

Audit

Receipt before claim

Runtime, package, policy, and result hashes keep the recovery result attached to the exact software, carrier, and control set that produced it.

Evidence boundary

Current support: controlled recovery and bounded emulator transport.

The public record supports Quantinuum SG Grand Challenge 2026 cohort participation, blinded recovery work, counts-only GF(2) recovery, and bounded H2-2E emulator transport. It does not establish physical-QPU execution, quantum advantage, a contest result, a production deployment, or a general Quantinuum partnership.
