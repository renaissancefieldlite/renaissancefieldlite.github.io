Licensing arc

From public evidence to a bounded partner integration.

01 Review the public architecture

Begin with the state-system page, companion map, support boundaries, and public repository rather than an unsupported capability claim.

02 Define the partner surface

Name the operating environment, state to preserve, evidence required, controls, constraints, and the result that would count as useful.

03 Open protected technical review

Claim-sensitive methods, implementation details, raw probes, and partner-specific system boundaries move into controlled review.

04 Integrate through measured gates

The partner build advances only when the declared evidence, security, acceptance, and deployment gates are satisfied.

What the architecture carries

State, memory, evidence, and control remain one operating thread.

State

Know what state the system is in

Interaction state, continuity state, source identity, uncertainty, and active task position remain explicit across models and tools.

Memory

Recover the causal path

Durable state, compaction records, corrections, unfinished obligations, and exact next gates support reliable recovery after interruption.

Evidence

Keep support attached to the claim

Receipts, source paths, controls, boundaries, and failed or open results stay with the state instead of disappearing behind a final answer.

Control

Advance only through accepted gates

Plans and mutations are bounded by declared acceptance conditions. Current hidden-state monitoring remains measurement-only; direct tuning is a separate gate.

Partner surfaces

The same proof discipline can travel into different operating environments.

Enterprise AI systems

Durable workflow state, evidence-aware automation, protected infrastructure, and recovery across long-running operations.

Research and model systems

Measured state paths, frozen controls, local evidence memory, and bounded model or adapter experiments.

Biological and device lanes

Quality-aware signal state, source timing, device context, and guarded routing through B.A.S.I.S. and related research surfaces.

Local and sovereign deployments

Cloud, private server, local workstation, edge hardware, and offline paths shaped around the partner's privacy and operational requirements.

Evidence boundary

Protected review does not turn proposed work into completed work.

Current public support covers durable AI state, evidence memory, recovery, acceptance-gated planning, aggregate state-path measurement, and external adapter lanes. A license defines access and scope; each partner deployment still requires its own implementation, controls, verification, and receipt.
